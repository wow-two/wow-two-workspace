# Content Formats

*Last updated: 2026-06-23*

> The short-form-video + content format library for demand-gen — formats that go viral, sell, and prove the product is useful. Where to post them → `channels.md` · recognizable meme/audio shells to pour these into → `meme-templates.md`.

## Principles

- **hook in the first 1-2s** (a visual + on-screen text) — the whole clip rides on it.
- one idea per clip · **<20-30s** · **show the aha** (the product actually doing the thing).
- **real screen-record or real people + trending audio > AI talking-head** (uncanny, off-tone) — use AI for B-roll only.
- CTA drives **branded search > clicks** → a memorable name + "search {brand}" beats "link in bio".
- **consistency > polish** — post near-daily; most clips flop, one hit carries.
- **repurpose one clip** → TikTok + Reels + Shorts (+ Pinterest); shoot once, post everywhere.

---

## Viral (reach + shares)

- **POV / overheard skit** — "Vanessa, what are you doing? just drop the PDF on the QR?" a real workflow, real people.
- **problem horror** — "they bricked my printed QR when I downgraded 💀" (the hostage-code wedge, emotional).
- **before / after (satisfying)** — ugly default QR → styled branded QR, screen-recorded.
- **"nobody tells you…" hot take** — QR codes expire + get held hostage on downgrade.
- **stitch / react** — react to a real QR fail (an expired menu · a 404 billboard code).
- **trend-jack** — the product demo dropped under a trending sound.
- **oddly-satisfying loop** — a QR restyling in real time (colors morph, logo drops in).

---

## Sell (intent → signup)

- **use-case demo (faceless)** — "a menu QR that swaps lunch↔dinner automatically."
- **"how to {task} in 30s"** — a WiFi QR for your Airbnb · a vCard · an app-store router.
- **comparison / vs** — "$30 QR Tiger vs a $1 code that never expires."
- **build-in-public / origin** — "I built this because a vendor bricked my printed code."
- **UGC / testimonial** — a real café owner shows their code on the table tent.
- **objection-buster** — "'QR codes are dead' — 1T scans in 2025…".

---

## Educate (trust · evergreen · SEO-adjacent)

- **tutorial series** — one per content type (vCard · WiFi · menu-by-time · app-store · GS1).
- **"5 QR mistakes that don't scan"** — quiet zone · contrast · size (repurpose the blog).
- **tips carousel** (IG, photo + music) — styling tips · use-cases; low-effort, no video.
- **mini case study** — "one food truck, 3 locations, one code."

---

## Production

- toolkit: screen-record the app + CapCut + a trending sound; a phone for skits.
- **faceless-friendly:** demos · before/after · loops · tutorials (no camera needed).
- **outsource:** pay UGC creators for skits/testimonials to scale without being on-camera.

---

## Metrics

- watch **saves + shares** (intent), completion rate, **branded-search lift**, and signup spikes after a post.
- put a UTM link in bio; ask "how'd you hear" at signup — short-form attribution is fuzzy.
