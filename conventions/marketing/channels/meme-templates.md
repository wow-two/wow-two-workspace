# Meme Templates

*Last updated: 2026-07-07*

> The reusable meme / cultural / trending-audio templates you pour a product demo into — the recognizable *shell*, not the format type (that's `content-formats.md`). Generic for any wow-two app; ForeverPin (QR) is the running example.

## How to use

- pick a **template** (durable) → ride a **current sound** on top (ephemeral — rotates weekly).
- **map the product to the shell:** dread→relief for a pain-fix wedge · reveal for a visual product · compare for a cheaper/better claim.
- **land the aha on the beat** — the product working (the QR restyles / routes) must hit the audio's drop or punchline.
- **one wedge per clip** (never-expire OR routing OR free-generator) · hook ≤2s · on-screen text always (most watch muted).

---

## Dread → relief (pain-fix / never-expire wedge)

- **"Oh no, oh no, oh no no no"** — build to the dread beat (code expired / vendor bricked it) → cut to the fix on the "no no no".
- **"Nobody's gonna know / they're gonna know"** — "print a QR that expires, nobody's gonna know… *they're gonna know*" → never-expire flip.
- **Anakin & Padmé ("for the better, right?")** — "it'll still work after I downgrade… right?" → silence → incumbent bricks it.
- **Two-button / sweating choice** — "reprint 10,000 flyers" vs "change the link in 2 taps".
- **Gru's plan (4-panel board)** — print QR → vendor hikes price → *code bricked* (record-scratch on the last panel).
- **How it started / how it's going** — printed 10k flyers → bricked code · vs · one code, reprogrammed forever.

---

## Reveal / flex (visual product shine)

- **"Wait for it"** — ugly default QR → *restyles into branded* on the beat, or a scan → routes to the right page.
- **Expanding / galaxy brain** — static QR → dynamic → dynamic + never-expire → one code routes by device/time.
- **Before / after** — default black QR → logo + gradient + frame (satisfying).
- **"It's the ___ for me"** — "it's the code that never expires for me."
- **Satisfying drag-drop** (screen ASMR) — drag a PDF onto the canvas → a styled QR pops out.

---

## Relatable / role (community + WOM)

- **"POV:"** — POV: your menu QR swaps lunch→dinner by itself at 4pm.
- **"Me explaining to {X} that…"** — explaining to the client we never reprint the sign.
- **"My {role} when…"** — my designer when the client changes the link *after* print.
- **"Nobody: / absolutely nobody: / me:"** — me making a QR for literally everything.
- **"Tell me you {X} without telling me you {X}"** — tell me you got burned by a QR vendor without telling me.
- **GRWM (get ready with me) while explaining** — GRWM while I set up the event's QR signage.

---

## Compare / debate (cheaper-better claim)

- **"Corporate needs you to find the difference" / "they're the same picture"** — $30 incumbent vs $1 never-expire → *not* the same picture.
- **"Change my mind"** (table setup) — "QR codes shouldn't expire. change my mind."
- **Distracted boyfriend** — an SMB eyeing ForeverPin while holding the incumbent's hand.
- **"He's a 10 but…"** — "she's a 10 but her QR code expired."

---

## Screen / tech-demo shells

- **speed-run "build X in 30s"** — fast-cut build + a ticking sound + a final live scan.
- **green-bubble reaction over a screen-record** — react in the corner while the demo plays.

---

## Sourcing current sounds

- **TikTok Creative Center → Trending → Sounds**; filter by region + last 7-30 days.
- pick **rising** sounds (≈10k-500k uses, still climbing) — not peaked (saturated) or tiny (no reach yet).
- keep a **swipe file** of 5-10 saved sounds/templates; batch-shoot against them.
- **the sound is ephemeral; the template is durable** — refresh sounds weekly, reuse these shells.

---

## Reuse

- generic across wow-two apps — swap the product; the ForeverPin lines are illustrative.
- match the **template's emotional arc to the product's wedge**; don't force a shell that fights the audio.
