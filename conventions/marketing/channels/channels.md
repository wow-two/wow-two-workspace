# Channels

*Last updated: 2026-06-23*

> The acquisition-channel catalog — every channel with its audience-fit, effort, and payoff for a bootstrapped SMB/prosumer micro-SaaS. Deep dives: `seo.md` · `content-formats.md`.

## Audience-fit rule

- match a channel to **who the users are**, not to what's easy or loud.
- **maker platforms (Product Hunt · IndieHackers · r/SideProject) reach BUILDERS, not end-users** → a launch bump + backlinks + credibility + feedback, **not** ICP acquisition for a horizontal SMB tool.
- two jobs: **capture** demand (SEO — people already searching) + **create** demand (short-form — people who don't know they need it).

## Catalog

| Channel | Fit (SMB tool) | Effort | Payoff lag | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SEO — content · comparison · pSEO | high (they search) | high | 6-12mo | **primary — capture** · `seo.md` |
| Short-form video — TikTok · Reels · Shorts | high (they scroll) | med, high-variance | days-mo | **primary — create** · `content-formats.md` |
| Pinterest | high (design/event/wedding search) | low-med | weeks | underrated — QR design inspiration + templates |
| YouTube — Shorts + how-to | med-high (2nd search engine) | med | months | evergreen tutorials, SEO longevity |
| Directories — AlternativeTo · G2 · Capterra | med (BOFU) | low | weeks | steady; submit once |
| Launch platforms — PH · IndieHackers | low (builders) | low-med | days | one-time bump + backlinks + credibility |
| Niche communities — subreddits · FB groups · Discord | high (where the pain lives) | med | weeks | seed + listen, never spam |
| Integrations / marketplaces — Zapier · Make · **Canva** | high (Canva = SMB designers) | med-high | weeks-mo | distribution once an API/app exists |
| Partnerships — print shops · POS · Etsy/wedding sellers | high (near the print moment) | med | months | co-distribution |
| Creator / UGC — pay creators to make clips | high | $ + coordination | weeks | scale short-form without being on-camera |
| Email / lifecycle (calm) | retention, not acquisition | low | immediate | keep, don't nag |
| Referral / word-of-mouth | high | low | slow | once retention is solid |
| Paid ads | poor at low ARPU | $$$ | immediate | skip early |

## Standouts for a QR/link tool

- **Canva app** — millions of SMB designers build flyers there → a QR app = native distribution to the exact ICP.
- **Wedding / event niche** — Pinterest + Etsy + r/weddingplanning; printable QR signs are a hot, high-intent sub-market.
- **Short-form** — the ICP (café · retail · realtor · marketer) scrolls at lunch; SEO can't create demand they don't yet search for.
