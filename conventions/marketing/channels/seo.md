# SEO

*Last updated: 2026-06-23*

> Search strategy for a crowded / commodity category — intents, comparison pages, programmatic SEO. Broader GTM → `../go-to-market.md`.

## Intents (target all three)

- **problem-aware / commercial** — "qr code generator", "dynamic qr code", "free qr code with logo".
- **comparison (BOFU, ~7.5% convert)** — "{incumbent} alternative", "dynamic vs static qr".
- **trust tutorials** — a how-to per content type (vCard · WiFi · menu-by-time).

---

## Comparison pages

- the fastest BOFU win; one distinct page per competitor.
- **"vs the manual / status-quo"** often beats "vs a competitor" for an early product — it meets the buyer at the moment of realizing the old way is painful.

---

## Programmatic SEO

- for combinatorial long-tail (use-case × type × feature).
- **quality gate:** ≥500 words + ~30% unique per page — thin content craters ~⅓ of pSEO implementations.
- topical authority = a cluster of genuinely useful pages + a few real backlinks, **not** page volume.
