# Go-To-Market

*Last updated: 2026-06-23*

> The GTM meta-playbook for a bootstrapped wow-two micro-SaaS — laws, launch sequence, activation, pricing, metrics, workflow. Per-channel depth lives in `channels/`.

## Laws

- distribution ≈ **70%** of the outcome — GTM is the lever, not more features.
- pick **1-2 primary channels** and master them — not scattershot.
- match the channel to the **audience**, not to what's loud — a horizontal SMB tool won't find its users on maker platforms (see `channels/channels.md`).
- run **both demand modes**: *capture* existing demand (SEO) + *create* demand where the ICP scrolls (short-form video).
- **retention-first** · **calm lifecycle** (more email can lower DAU/MAU) · **price for fee-efficiency** (annual for any sub-$3/mo plan).

## Channel map

- full catalog + audience-fit + effort/payoff → `channels/channels.md`.
- deep dives → `channels/seo.md` · `channels/content-formats.md`.
- default primary for a search-driven category: **SEO** (capture) **+ short-form video** (create); everything else supports.

---

## Launch sequence

- **pre-launch** (wks −4→0): analytics in · demo video/GIF · seed 3-5 communities · first content · schedule Product Hunt · a lead magnet.
- **launch**: IndieHackers + niche subreddits → Product Hunt (12:01 PT, comment-asks) → directories. Treat as a **bump + backlinks + credibility**, not ICP acquisition.
- **growth**: review CAC by channel · cut losers, double the winner · start the short-form cadence · pursue integrations/partnerships.

---

## Activation & retention

- define the **aha** (first real value) + a **magic number** (an action threshold that correlates with retention); onboard straight to it.
- **retention is the foundation** — measure cohort retention before scaling acquisition.

---

## Pricing & CRO

- free tier ≈ **70%** of paid value; free→paid **< 2%** signals a PMF problem.
- transparent flat pricing + an **annual option** (fee efficiency + commitment); a higher tier **anchors** the one you want sold.
- pricing page: one value-metric · comparison vs incumbents · FAQ · social proof.

---

## Metrics

- **privacy-respecting analytics** (Plausible-style) — no cookie banners, on-brand for a calm product.
- funnel: visit → signup/guest → **activation** → paid → retained; one number per stage.
- short-form drives **branded search > clicks** → also track branded-search volume + a "how'd you hear" at signup.

---

## Workflow

1. value-metric + aha. 2. free magnet + a fast activation path. 3. SEO foundation. 4. short-form cadence. 5. launch (IH/Reddit → PH → directories). 6. calm lifecycle. 7. measure, cut/scale by CAC. 8. integrations when an API exists.
